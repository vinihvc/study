If we list all the natural numbers **below** 10 that are multiples of 3 or 5, we get 3, 5, 6 and 9. The sum of these multiples is 23.

Find the sum of all the multiples of 3 or 5 below 1000.

## How to Solve

<Spoiler>

Iterate from 1 to `limit - 1` and add each number that is divisible by 3 or 5. Alternatively, use inclusion–exclusion: sum multiples of 3, plus multiples of 5, minus multiples of 15 (counted twice).

</Spoiler>
